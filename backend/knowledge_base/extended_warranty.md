# Extended Warranty Benefit – American Express

## Overview
Extended Warranty doubles the original manufacturer’s warranty period (up to a maximum extension) for eligible products purchased entirely with an eligible American Express Card, when the original warranty is 12 months or less (terms vary by market and product).

## Coverage Window
- Extends the manufacturer’s warranty by the same length as the original warranty, **up to 12 additional months** (prototype rule).
- Original manufacturer warranty must be valid in the country of purchase/use.
- Benefit applies only after the manufacturer’s warranty has expired.

## Eligible Cards
- The Platinum Card®
- American Express® Gold Card
- Select American Express Credit Cards

## Eligible Products
- Consumer electronics (laptops, phones, TVs, audio equipment)
- Home appliances with standard manufacturer warranties
- Watches and certain durable goods
- Products with a written manufacturer warranty of 24 months or less (eligibility rules apply)

## Coverage Limits
- Up to **₹1,00,000 per item**
- Maximum **₹3,00,000 per card member per year**
- Repair cost or replacement value at Amex’s option, not to exceed purchase price

## Conditions
- Product must be purchased new (not refurbished, unless manufacturer-certified and covered).
- Full purchase amount must be charged to the eligible Card.
- Manufacturer’s warranty must be registered if required by the manufacturer.
- Claim is for mechanical breakdown / covered failure after original warranty ends and during the extension period.

## Exclusions
- Products with lifetime warranties
- Motor vehicles and vehicle-installed equipment
- Consumables and perishable parts
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function
- Damage from misuse, abuse, or unauthorized modifications
- Software failures, viruses, and data loss
- Products used commercially
- Used, second-hand, or gray-market goods
- Batteries and wearable parts subject to normal depletion

## How to Claim
1. Contact manufacturer first if still under original warranty.
2. After original warranty expires, if failure occurs during extension period, open an Extended Warranty claim.
3. Provide proof of purchase, warranty documentation, and failure description.
4. Follow repair/replacement instructions from the claims administrator.

## Required Documents
- Original receipt and Card statement
- Manufacturer warranty card or terms
- Service diagnosis / repair estimate
- Serial number and model of the product
