# Purchase Protection Guide – American Express

## Overview
Purchase Protection is a complimentary benefit available on eligible American Express Cards. Items purchased entirely with an eligible Card are protected against accidental damage and theft for a limited period from the date of purchase.

## Coverage Window
- Eligible items are protected for **90 days** from the date of purchase.
- Coverage begins at the moment the purchase is completed with the eligible Card.
- Claims must be filed within **90 days** of the incident (damage or theft).

## Eligible Cards
- The Platinum Card®
- American Express® Gold Card
- American Express SmartEarn Credit Card (select regions)
- Centurion Card

## Eligible Items
- Electronics (laptops, smartphones, tablets, headphones, cameras, smartwatches)
- Home appliances (refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, microwaves)
- Furniture and home furnishings
- Clothing and accessories (with conditions; single-item value typically above ₹2,000)
- Sporting goods and fitness equipment
- Jewelry and watches (within specified sub-limits)

## Coverage Limits
- Up to **₹1,00,000 per occurrence** (prototype example limit)
- Maximum **₹5,00,000 per card member per calendar year**
- Deductible: None for Platinum; may apply for some card tiers
- Coverage is secondary to any manufacturer warranty or primary insurance

## What Is Covered
- Accidental damage (e.g., dropped laptop, water spill on electronics)
- Theft with evidence of forced entry or police report where applicable
- Damage during normal personal use within the coverage window

## Exclusions
- Consumables (food, beverages, toiletries, batteries)
- Motor vehicles, motorcycles, and watercraft
- Cash, gift cards, traveler’s cheques, and monetary instruments
- Items left unattended in public places
- Damage due to normal wear and tear, gradual deterioration, or insects/vermin
- Intentional damage or fraud
- Items purchased for commercial resale
- Software, digital goods, and downloadable content
- Plants, animals, and perishable goods
- Real estate and permanent fixtures

## How to Claim
1. Report the incident as soon as reasonably possible.
2. Gather: original receipt/invoice, Card statement showing the charge, photos of damage (if applicable), police report (for theft).
3. Submit a claim through the Benefit Activation Engine or Amex claims portal.
4. Provide additional documentation if requested by the claims administrator.

## Required Documents
- Proof of purchase (receipt or invoice showing merchant, date, amount, item description)
- Card statement reflecting the eligible charge
- Photographs of damaged item (damage claims)
- Police report or FIR (theft claims)
- Manufacturer warranty details if any
