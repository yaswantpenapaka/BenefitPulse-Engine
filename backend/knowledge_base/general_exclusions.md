# General Exclusions – American Express Card Benefits

## Purpose
This document lists exclusions that commonly apply across Purchase Protection, Return Protection, Extended Warranty, Travel Delay, and related Card Member benefits. Individual benefit certificates control in case of conflict; this guide is for prototype RAG grounding.

## Universal Exclusions
The following are generally **not covered** under Card benefit programs:

### Financial & Monetary Items
- Cash, currency, cryptocurrency
- Gift cards, prepaid cards, stored value instruments
- Stocks, bonds, securities, and negotiable instruments
- Lottery tickets and gambling-related purchases

### Vehicles & Transport Assets
- Automobiles, motorcycles, scooters
- Boats, aircraft, and drones (unless a specific rider applies)
- Vehicle parts permanently installed
- Fuel and EV charging (not a protection benefit)

### Consumables & Perishables
- Food and beverages
- Cosmetics and toiletries (often excluded or limited)
- Medicines and medical devices (except where a specific health benefit applies)
- Plants, flowers, animals

### Digital & Intangible
- Software licenses and subscriptions
- Streaming services and digital downloads
- Domain names and cloud services
- NFTs and purely digital collectibles

### Services
- Professional services (legal, medical, consulting fees)
- Memberships and club fees (except where a specific credit applies)
- Travel services already covered/excluded under separate travel terms

### Conduct-Related
- Intentional damage, fraud, or criminal acts by the Card Member
- Losses due to war, invasion, civil war, or nuclear hazard
- Confiscation by government authority
- Normal wear and tear

### Commercial Use
- Inventory purchased for resale
- Equipment used primarily for business operations (unless card is a business product with explicit coverage)
- Rental property furnishings in some markets

## Time-Based Failures
- Claims filed after the stated filing deadline
- Incidents outside the coverage window (e.g., after 90 days for Purchase Protection)
- Pre-existing damage before purchase

## Documentation Failures
Benefits may be denied if the Card Member cannot provide:
- Proof that the full eligible amount was charged to an eligible Card
- Sufficient evidence of purchase, ownership, or incident
- Required police reports for theft where mandated

## Coordination of Benefits
- Card benefits are often **secondary** to manufacturer warranties, home insurance, and travel insurance.
- Duplicate recovery of the same loss from multiple sources is not permitted beyond actual loss.
