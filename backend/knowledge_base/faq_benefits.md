# FAQ – American Express Card Benefits

## General

### What is the Benefit Activation Engine?
It automatically monitors eligible Card transactions, detects purchases that may qualify for protections (such as Purchase Protection or Travel Delay), and prepares a pre-filled claim for you to review and confirm.

### Do I need to enroll?
Purchase Protection, Return Protection, and Extended Warranty are typically automatic for eligible Cards when you pay with the Card. Some travel benefits may require the ticket to be charged to the Card. Always check your Card’s terms.

### Is there a fee to use these benefits?
Core protection benefits listed here are complimentary features of eligible American Express Cards. Claims administration does not charge a filing fee in this prototype model.

## Purchase Protection

### How long am I covered after buying electronics?
For eligible items, Purchase Protection typically covers accidental damage and theft for **90 days** from purchase when paid with an eligible Card.

### My laptop was damaged 45 days after purchase. Can I claim?
Yes, if the item is eligible, purchased on an eligible Card, the damage is accidental (not wear and tear), and you are within the claim filing window. You will need the receipt, statement, and photos.

### Are phones covered?
Yes, smartphones are generally eligible electronics under Purchase Protection, subject to exclusions and limits.

## Return Protection

### The store only allows 7-day returns. What can I do?
If the item is eligible and still within the Amex Return Protection window (typically 90 days), you may be able to use Return Protection after the merchant refuses or their policy expires.

## Travel Delay

### My flight was delayed 5 hours. What can I claim?
If your ticket was charged to an eligible Card and the delay meets the policy threshold (often 4+ hours), you may claim reasonable meals, and if needed, lodging and essential items, within limits.

### Do I need a delay certificate?
Yes. Obtain official confirmation from the airline and keep receipts for expenses caused by the delay.

## Extended Warranty

### Does Extended Warranty start immediately?
No. It typically begins **after** the manufacturer’s warranty ends and extends coverage for a defined additional period (up to 12 months in this prototype).

### Is a 3-year manufacturer warranty extended?
Many programs only extend warranties of a certain length (e.g., 24 months or less). Check the Extended Warranty certificate for your Card.

## Claims Process

### How pre-filled is my claim?
The engine pre-fills merchant, amount, date, Card, benefit type, coverage window, policy reference, and explanation. You usually only need to upload a receipt and confirm details.

### What is a confidence score?
It reflects how sure the system is that the transaction matches an eligible benefit, based on merchant normalization, category match, rules evaluation, and policy retrieval.

### Can I talk to an assistant about my claim?
Yes. The in-app Assistant can answer questions about eligibility, required documents, and policy terms using the benefits knowledge base, and can cite relevant policy sections.

## Exclusions Quick Hits

### Is fuel covered by Purchase Protection?
No. Fuel and many consumables are excluded.

### Are restaurant meals covered by Purchase Protection?
No. Food and beverages are consumables and excluded from Purchase Protection.

### What about Amazon purchases?
Eligible durable goods bought on Amazon with an eligible Card can qualify for Purchase Protection if they meet category and exclusion rules (e.g., a laptop yes; groceries no).
