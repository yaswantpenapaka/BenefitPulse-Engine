# Travel Delay Insurance – American Express

## Overview
Travel Delay Insurance provides reimbursement for reasonable additional expenses incurred when a covered trip is delayed due to covered reasons, when the Card Member has charged eligible travel costs to an eligible American Express Card.

## Coverage Window
- Applies to common carrier delays (airline, rail, etc.) typically of **4 hours or more** (or overnight delay as defined in policy).
- Coverage is tied to the trip for which eligible tickets were purchased with the Card.
- Claim must be filed within **30 days** of the delay incident (or as specified in your Card’s terms).

## Eligible Cards
- The Platinum Card®
- American Express® Gold Card (select markets)
- Centurion Card

## What Triggers Coverage
- Covered common carrier delay of the scheduled departure
- Weather-related delays
- Equipment failure of the common carrier
- Strike or industrial action (subject to exclusions)
- Lost or delayed luggage causing essential purchases (sub-limit may apply under related benefits)

## Covered Expenses (examples)
- Meals and refreshments during the delay
- Hotel accommodation if overnight delay is necessary
- Essential toiletries and clothing if baggage is delayed
- Local transportation to/from hotel and airport/station

## Coverage Limits
- Up to **₹20,000 per person per delay event** (prototype example)
- Sub-limits may apply for meals, lodging, and essential items
- Maximum **₹1,00,000 per card member per year** for travel delay benefits combined (example)

## Eligible Travel Purchases
- Airline tickets charged in full to the eligible Card
- Rail tickets for long-distance travel charged to the Card
- Package travel where the transport component is charged to the Card (subject to terms)

## Exclusions
- Delays under 4 hours (or under the policy’s minimum delay threshold)
- Delays known to the Card Member before booking or departure
- Failure to check in as required
- War, nuclear risks, and certain civil disturbances
- Expenses that would have been incurred regardless of the delay
- First-class upgrades or luxury expenses beyond reasonable necessity
- Business travel expenses reimbursable by employer (coordination of benefits may apply)

## How to Claim
1. Obtain delay confirmation from the airline/carrier (delay certificate or written notice).
2. Keep all receipts for additional expenses incurred due to the delay.
3. Submit claim with ticket proof (Card charge), delay documentation, and expense receipts.
4. Provide itinerary and boarding passes where available.

## Required Documents
- Proof of ticket purchase on eligible Card (statement + booking confirmation)
- Carrier delay certificate or official delay communication
- Itemized receipts for claimed expenses
- Travel itinerary
