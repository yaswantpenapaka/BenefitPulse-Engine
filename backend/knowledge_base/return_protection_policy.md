# Return Protection Policy – American Express

## Overview
Return Protection helps Card Members when a merchant’s return policy is more restrictive than this benefit. If an eligible item cannot be returned to the merchant, American Express may reimburse the purchase price (subject to limits) when the item is returned under this program.

## Coverage Window
- Items must be returned under this benefit within **90 days** of the purchase date.
- The merchant must have refused the return, or the merchant’s return window must have expired while the Amex Return Protection window is still open.

## Eligible Cards
- American Express Platinum Card
- American Express Gold Card
- Select co-branded American Express Cards

## Eligible Items
- New merchandise purchased entirely with an eligible American Express Card
- Items that are unused, undamaged, and in original packaging when required
- Apparel, accessories, electronics accessories, home goods, and similar consumer products

## Coverage Limits
- Up to **₹50,000 per item**
- Maximum **₹2,00,000 per card member per calendar year**
- Shipping costs for return to Amex/administrator may be reimbursed or prepaid as per program rules

## Conditions
- Item must not be returnable to the original merchant (refusal or expired merchant policy).
- Item must still be within the Amex 90-day Return Protection window.
- Item must be in resalable condition unless damage is the reason merchant refused (subject to review).
- Original packaging preferred; packaging requirements may vary by product category.

## Exclusions
- Items returned for cash, store credit, or exchange already completed with merchant
- Perishable goods, food, beverages
- Motor vehicles and related accessories permanently installed
- Jewelry, precious metals, rare coins (unless explicitly covered under a card-specific rider)
- Tickets (events, travel), gift cards, and services
- Custom-made or personalized items
- Items purchased for commercial use or resale
- Digital downloads and software licenses
- Items damaged by the Card Member intentionally

## How to Claim
1. Attempt return with the original merchant first; retain written refusal if available.
2. Confirm the item is within 90 days of purchase.
3. Submit claim with receipt, Card statement, and item details.
4. Ship the item as instructed by the claims administrator after approval of claim initiation.

## Required Documents
- Original receipt or invoice
- Card statement showing the purchase
- Merchant return policy (screenshot or printout) and/or written refusal
- Photos of item and packaging
